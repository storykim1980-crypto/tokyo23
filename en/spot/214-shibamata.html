Local Knowledge

Obvious to locals, invisible to visitors

There is a simple reason guidebooks leave this out: to the people who live here it is too obvious to be worth explaining.

団
How to order
The approach is lined with kusa-dango shops

The approach is lined with kusa-dango shops — Takagiya, Toraya, Yoshinoya — and the local way is to buy one skewer at each and compare. Around ¥150 a stick. Toraya grills its version only at weekends and holidays.

寅
The backstory
Toraya, founded 1887, played Tora-san&#x27;s family home in the first four Otoko wa Tsurai

Toraya, founded 1887, played Tora-san&#x27;s family home in the first four Otoko wa Tsurai yo films. It was renamed from Shibamataya in 1989, and the staircase and old posters are still there.

彫
Unwritten rule
The carved Lotus Sutra scenes on the gate and main hall are the real substance here

The carved Lotus Sutra scenes on the gate and main hall are the real substance here. The grounds are free but the carving gallery and garden are ticketed. Behind the temple sit Yamamoto-tei and the Tora-san museum.

渡
Local timing
Walk past the shops to the Edogawa and you will find the Yagiri ferry still running

Walk past the shops to the Edogawa and you will find the Yagiri ferry still running — one of the last in Tokyo. At weekends the floodplain fills with junior baseball practice.

What it is

Katsushika is a ward where Shibamata and old-fashioned warmth remain. Midday and dusk leave completely different impressions. Come at the wrong hour and it is just an ordinary block. Toilets are far off. Sort that out at a station or park beforehand. Arrive at opening and seating is no issue — though turnover is brisk, so lingering is hard. Map apps drift in the narrow lanes; for the last hundred metres, look for the sign instead. The more times someone has been to Tokyo, the longer they tend to stay in places like this.

What to order

草だんご
Takagiya, Toraya, Yoshinoya and others. Buying one from each and comparing is how the street is done.
About ¥150 per skewer
Lotus Sutra relief carvings
The carvings of the Nitenmon gate and Taishakudo. The grounds are free; the gallery is not.
Separate charge
矢切の渡し
Down at the river, a ferry still runs — one of the very few left in Tokyo.
¥200 approx.

Why locals keep coming

It costs nothing, so it fits any itinerary regardless of budget. It is closer to seeing the city&#x27;s reverse side — nothing like a manicured observation deck.
